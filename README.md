# Ghumo

A clickable prototype of a pocket city guide for Ahmedabad. Kesar, a stray red cat, points you to one odd detail at each place. You snap it, and the find becomes a postmarked stamp in your book.

**Try it:** https://evanaforai-dev.github.io/ghumo/

## Controls

- **Roller, or ↑ ↓:** scroll
- **OK, or space:** open, go, snap
- **Back, or esc:** back

You can also tap anything on the screen.

## Notes

- Your progress (stamps, photos, screen mode) is saved in your own browser.
- Distances are measured from Navrangpura until real GPS is switched on.
- "On now" is sample data.
